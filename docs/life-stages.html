Liraen Life Stages

1. Merling (Child) — ages 0–10

Physical Traits

  • Shorter tails with rounder flukes
  • Larger eyes for learning and curiosity
  • Glow patterns extremely bright and unpredictable
  • Hair short or softly floating

Behavior

  • Communicate with chirps, trills, and bright glow bursts
  • Learn gesture‑grammar before harmonic tones
  • Often race each other through kelp forests

Cultural Note

Merlings are considered “living lanterns” and are fiercely protected.

2. Adolescent Liraen — ages 10–18

Physical Traits

  • Tails elongate rapidly
  • Glow begins stabilizing into adult patterns
  • Voice develops a harmonic “second tone”
  • Males deepen in resonance; females gain clearer upper tones

Behavior

  • Begin formal Pelagium training
  • Participate in story‑circles and current‑chants
  • Show early artistic or navigational specialties

Cultural Note

This stage is called “the Shifting” — their glow flickers between childhood chaos and adult control.

3. Adult Liraen — ages 18–80

Physical Traits

  • Tail fully formed; shape and color reflect family lineage
  • Hair reaches its final color spectrum
  • Glow patterns become unique identifiers, like fingerprints
  • Full harmonic voice (2–3 tones at once)

Behavior

  • Sing long‑form story‑songs
  • Serve roles as navigators, singers, hunters, lorekeepers, and linguists
  • Courtship rituals involve glow‑synchronization and harmonic duets

Cultural Note

Adults are defined by their role within pod or reef society.

4. Elder Liraen — ages 80+

Physical Traits

  • Skin develops a pearlescent sheen
  • Glow shifts to whites, soft golds, and pale blues
  • Hair fades to silver or glowing white
  • Tails broaden but lose some power

Behavior

  • Retire from hunting
  • Become “Deep Listeners,” specializing in ancestral memory
  • Transmit long‑distance songs
  • Teach Pelagium to younglings

Cultural Note

Elders hold sacred status; their words are considered literal “light in the water.”
