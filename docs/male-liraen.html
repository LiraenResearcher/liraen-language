Male Liraen

Overall Aesthetic

Male Liraen possess a more solid, grounded presence — often described as “current‑shapers” due to their deeper, resonant movements and the way they influence the flow of water around them.

Body Form

  • Slightly broader shoulders and more muscular torsos
  • Shorter but more powerful fin‑paddles built for explosive bursts of speed
  • Fin edges are simpler but thicker — designed for strength rather than display

Glow Patterns

  • More muted hues: deep blue, teal, and soft white
  • Glow intensifies during:
    • warnings
    • protective displays
    • ritual chanting
    • territorial dances

Facial Structure

  • Sharper cheekbones
  • Stronger brow ridges

Voice

  • Deep harmonic undertones
  • Capable of producing subsonic pulses used for:
    • communication over vast distances
    • calming distressed podmates
    • intimidating predators
